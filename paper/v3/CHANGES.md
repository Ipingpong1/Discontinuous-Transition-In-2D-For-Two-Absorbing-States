## Matters deliberately left open

No additional simulation campaign was required by the review. Establishing an asymptotic generic-coexistence interval would need matched physical strip geometries and multiple droplet radii inside the candidate interval. Resolving a precise five-cell coexistence boundary would need an intact retreating slab or another identifiable front observable. The endpoint transition order, strict continuous-time limit, and wetting divergence remain open research questions. Author metadata and a public archive identifier require information from the authors and are not invented in this draft.

# Strip crossings at p = 0.99 and 0.995 beyond the crossover time (`strip_p099/`)

Question (referee reports of 2026-10-29/30, point M3; `paper/main.tex` Table I footnote b and
Sec. V B): the strip-front crossings that give q*(0.99) = 0.00128 and q*(0.995) = 0.00062 in Table I
come from runs of T = 1e5 steps (E4_voter, 2 seeds, Lx 2048 x Ly 4096; p = 0.995 from a local scan,
2 seeds, Lx = Ly = 2048), shorter than the wall crossover time t_x ~ 3e5 (p = 0.99) and ~ 4e5
(p = 0.995) of Sec. V B, while the bulk active phase at p = 0.995 is stationary only for
q >= 1.035 q*. The paper therefore declares q* and c at p = 0.995 lower bounds. Here the same strip
protocol is rerun to T = 1.2e6 (p = 0.99, 4 t_x) and T = 1.6e6 (p = 0.995, 4 t_x) with 4 seeds per
point, the same geometry as before plus Lx 4096, and the bulk active phase is checked for
stationarity between the old q* and 1.035 q*.

Status: Secs. 1-3 were written before launch (30 Oct 2026, 23:49) and are left as written; Sec. 3.1-3.2 record
the two extensions made during the run; Secs. 4-9 are the results (31 Oct). The last runs (tall box,
bulk L 4096) finished at 13:08; every table is regenerated by `python3 sec_tables.py > tables.md`
(compact) and `python3 analyze.py all` (`analysis.md`, every estimator and window); `strips.png` by
`python3 plot_strips.py`.

## 1. Crossover time, from the paper's own statements

Sec. V B (`main.tex` l. 645-654) and `campaign/README.md` (E8b results): a flat wall between the two
absorbing phases roughens like a voter interface until a crossover time t_x, after which q takes
over; t_x = the time from which W(1.05 q*)/W(0.98 q*) stays above 1.5: ~ 8e3 (p 0.9), 4e4 (0.98),
3e5 (0.99), 4e5 (0.995), roughly t_x ~ (1 - p)^-1.3. The power law from p = 0.9 gives 2e5 (0.99)
and 4.9e5 (0.995). The lazy-front test (E11_lazy_front_p99, README Phase 7) showed the same at the
strip level: at p = 0.99, r = 1/4, fronts just above q*(r) retreat for ~ 1e5 physical units before
advancing, so a crossing from a <= 1e5 window is a transient. Taking the larger of the two
estimates at each p: **t_x ~ 3e5 (p = 0.99), ~ 4-5e5 (p = 0.995)**. The E4 strips (T = 1e5) stopped
at 1/3 and 1/4 of t_x.

## 2. Protocol

Code: the root `dnsim` v1.3 (`/home/mo2273/Desktop/dn/dnsim`, the binary of the near-voter campaigns
E6-E8; selftest passed before the runs). Same model as Table I: `--rule B3678/S34678 --alpha 0.5
--voter p --jflip 2:q`.

(i) Strips, E4_voter protocol: `--init strip:0.5:0.5 --bg 0 --stop-absorbed --ppd 10 --profile`,
profiles every 2000 steps (E4: 1000) plus 10 per decade, Lx 2048 x Ly 4096, 4 seeds (1-4, the same
seeds at every q, so the per-seed v(q) curves share their random streams), checkpoints every 5e4
steps (`--ckpt --resume`).
- p = 0.995: q = 0.00058, 0.00060, 0.00062, 0.00064, 0.00066 (x = q/0.00062 = 0.935, 0.968, 1.0,
  1.032, 1.065; the bulk threshold of E6_long_p995 lies between 0.000632 and 0.000642), T = 1.6e6.
- p = 0.99: q = 0.00122, 0.00125, 0.00128, 0.00131, 0.00134 (x = q/0.00128 = 0.953, 0.977, 1.0,
  1.023, 1.047; the E6_long bulk threshold lies between 0.00127 and 0.001283), T = 1.2e6.
- Larger L: Lx 4096 x Ly 4096 at p = 0.995, q = 0.00062 and 0.00064, 2 seeds, T = 1.6e6.
(ii) Bulk, E6_long protocol: `--init random:0.5 --L 2048 --every 1000 --ppd 20 --stop-absorbed`,
seeds 3-4 (E6 has seeds 1-2 at 0.97, 1.0, 1.01, 1.02, 1.035, 1.05, 1.1, 1.2 x q*):
- p = 0.995: q = 0.00063, 0.000636, 0.00064 (x = 1.016, 1.026, 1.032), T = 1.5e6; plus L 4096 at
  0.00064, seed 3, T = 1.5e6.
- p = 0.99: q = 0.00128, 0.00130 (x = 1.0, 1.016), T = 1.2e6.

Velocities. `fronts.py` tracks the two strip edges (half of the interior interface density, 32-row
smoothing) and fits W(t) over t >= 2e3 while W is in [0.15, 0.85] Ly and the centre is above
0.7 of its t ~ 1e3 value; that is the Table I estimator and is reproduced here for comparison
("early", t in [2e3, 1e5]). The measurement of this note is the same W(t) fitted in late windows
only, [t_x, T] = [3e5, 1.2e6] (p = 0.99) and [4e5, 1.6e6] (p = 0.995), per run, seed mean +- sem
(`vwin.py` style), with the window shortened where the strip leaves [0.15, 0.85] Ly. The interior
(central 256 rows) is followed to T and a point is "stationary" if its interior holds, "gone" if the
strip dissolves. q* is the zero of a weighted line through the late-window v of the stationary points
nearest the crossing (the Molofsky Sec. 8 recipe); the error is the seed bootstrap of that crossing
plus the spread between estimators (late window vs. whole run after t_x, Lx 2048 vs. 4096). c =
q*/[alpha (1 - p)] with alpha = 1/2.

1. Run to 4 t_x with 4 seeds, the strip-front crossing at p = 0.995 is q* = 0.000640(5), 3.2% above
   the Table I value 0.00062 (which came from 2 seeds at T = 1e5, a third of t_x); at p = 0.99 it is
   0.001282(8), the Table I value. Both are measurements now, not lower bounds: v(q) is linear
   through zero in the late window, strips below q* dissolve and strips above it grow ballistically.

   in Table I and Fig. 7 was the finite-time bias.

   x_+ = 1.00, and decays 0.6-1.5% below it. The strip crossing and the bulk threshold now agree
   to better than 1% at p = 0.995 (they disagreed by 2-3.5% in the paper); at p = 0.99 they agreed
   already. The finite-time strip crossing was the cause, as footnote b and Sec. V B supposed.

I read the PDF and its LaTeX source, then checked the claims against your own data (data/runs, data/figdata), the theory outputs in campaign/theory, and dnsim.c. Helper agents also read the key cited papers and searched for prior art. Numbers below come from your files unless I say otherwise.

Verdict

The central claim, a discontinuous transition at p = 1/2, is probably right. At 0.9987 q* all four seeds give a slowly retreating front (v = −0.0004 ± 0.0001), while the bulk at the same q stays stationary for L ≥ 1024. That is real bistability, and the bulk density just above q* is independent of L to about 10⁻⁴. The simulator is also sound: an independent naive implementation matched it bit for bit.

The paper oversells in four places a referee will attack:
- the "parameter-free" agreement between theory and simulation;
- the metastable branch below q*;
- several "hallmarks" that don't distinguish first order from a continuous transition with diffusive scaling;
- the claims at p ≥ 0.99.

There are also about a dozen checkable errors in the text. I'd expect "major revision."
